What the same life costs later

 
Each line is one category, inflated at the rate set against it and counted only in the years it runs. Nothing here assumes your habits change — it is the same life, repriced and switched on and off. Loan EMIs are fixed in rupee terms, so in today’s money they visibly shrink until they stop altogether. The general inflation figure beside the ages is only used to convert to today’s money; each row still inflates at its own rate.

Life-stage spending

 
Bar length is time, not money. Each bar marks the stretch of your life an expense applies to, laid on a track that runs the full span of the plan — a long bar means a long run, not a large bill. What it costs is in the column on the right. The curve above is the sum of whatever is running in a given year, which is why it steps down as school fees finish and instalments end. Hover any bar for what it costs and what it adds up to over its run, on whichever money basis you have selected above.
