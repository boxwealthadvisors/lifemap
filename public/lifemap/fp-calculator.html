LifeMap is an educational planning tool from BOX Wealth Advisors. The financial-freedom age is an illustration based on the assumptions you enter, not a forecast or a recommendation. It uses salary, work-asset streams and family-member income, each with its own growth rate and end age; personal assets are not sold to fund the plan; a holding you gave a contracted maturity value pays exactly that amount on its maturity date rather than the assumed return, and the proceeds are then reinvested with the rest; loan instalments repay the balance without interest; and your spending pattern holds in real terms. It does not model tax, healthcare shocks or market sequence risk. Past performance is not indicative of future results.
