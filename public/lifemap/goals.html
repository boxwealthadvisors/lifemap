Inflation rates are long-run Indian averages for each kind of spending, filled in as a starting point and fully editable. Private higher education has run at roughly 10–12% a year against headline inflation of 5–6%, and the published CPI education series understates this because it averages in government institutions. Weddings and property track construction and gold rather than the consumer basket. Use your own figure wherever you have one.

Spread over matters more than it looks. A four-year degree is not one bill in one year; each year is priced separately, and the later years cost more. Setting it to 1 will understate a multi-year commitment.

On double counting: anything you pay every year — school fees, insurance premiums, instalments — belongs in Expenses or Loans, not here. This register is for the lumpy, dated commitments that ordinary monthly spending never covers.
