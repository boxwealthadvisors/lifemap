What you earn

Your income streams beyond regular salary can be valuable work assets — each one is worth what it will still pay you before it stops. Set what each pays this year, how fast it grows and the age it ends, and the mix and the curve below rebuild themselves. Salary and annual bonuses belong in active employment income, not in this register.

Every stream runs from this age until the end age you give it. Change it and the whole page recalculates.
