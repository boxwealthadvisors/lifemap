LifeMap is an educational planning tool from BOX Wealth Advisors. The financial-freedom age is an illustration based on the assumptions you enter, not a forecast or a recommendation. It assumes salary is your only income and continues only until the working age selected, personal assets are not sold to fund the plan, loan instalments repay the balance without interest, and your spending pattern holds in real terms. It does not model tax, healthcare shocks or market sequence risk. Past performance is not indicative of future results.
