Path to debt-free

 
Each line is one loan, amortised at its own rate and instalment. Planned loans are drawn dashed, starting the year you give them. In the balance view the figure beside each name is the most that loan ever reaches, since every line ends at zero. Interest is charged on the balance before each payment is applied, so a loan whose instalment is smaller than its monthly interest never falls — it climbs. Nothing here assumes prepayment, a rate change or a missed month.

Ends in and instalment decide each other: type the year your loan runs to and LifeMap works out the instalment that clears it by then; change the instalment, the balance or the rate and the end year moves to match. Type whichever one your sanction letter actually states. If an instalment is smaller than one period’s interest the loan never clears, the end year is left blank and the row is flagged. Rates shown against each loan type are typical Indian market rates for early 2026 and are a starting point only; use the rate on your own sanction letter.

On double counting: instalments live here, not in Expenses. LifeMap counts them once, from this page, in every projection. If you have also entered an EMI row under Expenses, remove it there.
